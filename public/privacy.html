Privacy policy for Rejseappen

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This is an English translation of the Danish privacy policy. In case of any discrepancy, the Danish version applies.

As the app is run by me as a private individual with no associated company, I am personally the data controller for the personal data processed in connection with your use of the app.

2. What information we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we process the following information:

  • Email address and a password (the password is only stored in encrypted form by Firebase Authentication — we cannot see it).
  • Your trips, which you enter yourself: title, dates, destinations and places for each day, transport (e.g. company, flight or train number, times, seat and booking number) and stays (e.g. name, address, times, booking number, phone number and, if any, door code and Wi-Fi details).
  • Boarding passes, if you scan them: an image of the boarding pass and the information contained in the barcode (including the passenger's name, booking number, route and seat).
  • Photos you upload, including the information stored in the photo (the time it was taken and, if present, its GPS location).
  • Location data, if you actively choose it: GPS tracks from your phone, manual check-ins and tracks you import from a GPX file (see section 2a).
  • Friends and sharing: who you have added as a friend, and who you share a trip with.

The information is collected directly from you. You decide how much you enter — apart from the email address, none of the information is necessary to use the app.

2a. About GPS tracking and location

The app can track your route while you travel. Tracking is turned off by default and only runs while you have actively started it and have the app open. You can also check in at a place or import a track from e.g. a watch. Location data is used to show your route on the map and in the summary of the trip.

Tracking can be stopped at any time, and saved points and imported tracks can be deleted in the app.

2b. Who can see your information in the app

Your email address can be seen by other users who add you as a friend (this is how people find each other). A trip can only be seen by the people the trip's creator has shared it with. The trip's days, transport and stays can be seen and edited by everyone the trip is shared with. Photos and GPS tracks can only be seen by others if the trip's creator has turned on sharing of them — otherwise only you and the trip's creator see them. Boarding passes you have saved with a flight can be seen by the people the trip is shared with.

3. Purpose of the processing

We process your information in order to:

  • create and manage your user account and let you log in
  • store and show your trips so you can plan and document them
  • share trips with the travel companions you choose yourself
  • look up flights by flight number and date and find places on the map when you ask for it (see section 5)
  • be able to contact you about important matters concerning your account or the app

We do not sell your information or pass it on to third parties for marketing purposes. The app does not send newsletters.

4. Legal basis for the processing

  • Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation (performance of a contract): We need your email to provide the login function, and the trip information you enter yourself to provide the app's core function — storing, showing and sharing your trips.
  • Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation (consent): We only process GPS location data if you actively start tracking, check in or import a track. You can withdraw your consent at any time by stopping tracking and deleting the saved points.

If you enter information about other people (e.g. travel companions' names on a boarding pass), you must make sure that they agree to it.

5. Who has access to your information (data processors and other recipients)

Firebase (Google) is used to run the app's backend: login, database, storage of photos and boarding passes, and flight lookups. Google acts as a data processor on our behalf and only processes data according to our instructions. We have entered into Google's standard "Data Processing and Security Terms" (Firebase/Google Cloud DPA), which meet the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation for data processing agreements. Data is stored on servers in Europe.

When you use certain features, limited information is sent to these services:

  • AeroDataBox (via RapidAPI): When you tap "Get flight details" or scan a boarding pass, the flight number and date — not your name or other personal data — are sent in order to fetch airports and times.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: When you search for a place, the text you search for is sent. When the map is shown, map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap. In both cases the service receives your IP address, as with ordinary use of a website.

Beyond this, there are no other recipients of your information unless required by law.

6. Transfers to third countries

Google/Firebase may in certain cases process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the USA. Such transfers are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC), which Google has adopted, cf. Article 46 of the General Data Protection Regulation. The same applies to RapidAPI, which receives the flight number and date in flight lookups.

You can read more about Google's/Firebase's data protection terms at: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as you have an active account in the app. You can delete individual trip data yourself in the app (e.g. transport, stays, photos, boarding passes and GPS tracks).

If you want your account and all your information deleted, you can contact us at bsklausen@proton.me. We delete your personal data without undue delay and no later than within 30 days, unless we are legally obliged to keep certain information for longer. Trips you have shared with others are also deleted for them if you created the trip.

8. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • access the information we process about you
  • have incorrect information corrected
  • have your information deleted ("the right to be forgotten")
  • have the processing restricted in certain cases
  • object to the processing
  • data portability – receive your information in a structured, commonly used format
  • withdraw your consent to location data at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out before it was withdrawn

If you want to exercise one or more of these rights, you can contact us at bsklausen@proton.me.

You also have the right to complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) if you are dissatisfied with the way we process your information:
Datatilsynet, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark – www.datatilsynet.dk

9. Security

We have taken appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration or deletion, including:

  • encryption of data in transit (HTTPS)
  • access rules in the database, so a trip can only be read by the people it is shared with
  • use of Firebase Authentication for secure login handling

Preferably do not store information in the app that would be harmful if it fell into the wrong hands (e.g. passwords for other services).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, e.g. if the app develops or the legislation changes. In the event of significant changes, you will be asked to read and accept the policy again the next time you open the app.
